PA R T 1 · F O R E V E RYO N E

Care Copilot in plain words
Many older people want to stay in their own home. Their children want to help, but live
far away, work full time and get lost in paperwork. Care Copilot connects the two with as
little effort as possible.
A short story
 1      Maria (82) lives alone in Stuttgart. She has a tablet on her kitchen table with only one big
        button. When she presses it, a friendly voice greets her by name: "Hallo Maria, wie geht es
        Ihnen heute?"
 2      She simply talks. "I can't carry the shopping anymore, and I'm worried because the woman
        who helps me has no proper contract." No menus, no typing, no passwords.
 3      The AI helps where it can. It answers simple questions, reminds her of her medicine and keeps
        her company. Here it understands that Maria needs real help and that there is a legal problem,
        so it creates a help request.
 4      Her daughter Anna gets a message on her phone with a short summary of the call. She opens
        her dashboard.
 5      The dashboard tells Anna exactly what to do: the current home help setup is risky, the family is
        missing money it is entitled to, and here are the steps, one by one. It also shows helpers nearby
        who are verified and can be paid partly by the care insurance.
 6      Anna books a helper in two taps. Maria gets help with the shopping. Anna stops worrying about
        doing something illegal.

When does the AI hand over to a human?
This rule lives in the code, not in the AI's judgement, so it is predictable:
  The senior asks for a person, or the AI is not sure it understood.
  The topic is legal (contracts, carers, letters from authorities) or money (benefits, bills).
  Something sounds urgent: a fall, pain, confusion, no food at home. The family gets an immediate
  alert.

  Safety first: the assistant is not an emergency service. If the senior describes an emergency, it tells them to
  call 112 and alerts the family at the same time.

Connecting ElevenLabs in six steps
1. Create an agent from the blank template. First message: "Hallo {{senior_name}}, wie geht es Ihnen
   heute?". Enable German and Italian.
2. In the app, use useConversation from @elevenlabs/react and ask for microphone access with a
   clear explanation.
3. Keep the API key on the server: /api/signed-url fetches a signed URL for the browser.
4. Add client tools: create_help_request(category, summary) and notify_family(urgency, message) ,
   set to blocking so the agent waits for the result.
5. In the agent's Analysis tab add data fields: mood, problem_category, needs_human, summary.
6. Add a post-call webhook to /api/voice-webhook : save the session, apply the escalation rule, email
   the family.
