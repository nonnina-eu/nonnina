## The five slides the jury asks for

1. **Problem** – *who* exactly: adult children 40–60 living away from a parent 70+, full-time job, coordinating care over calls, WhatsApp and Google. Why it sucks today: seniors cannot use apps, benefits/legal/providers are a maze, help goes unused.
2. **Solution** – one sentence: *Nonnina is a voice-first AI care companion that connects seniors, their families and trusted local helpers.* Why 10×: one button instead of apps for the senior; one place instead of five channels for the family; every spoken need ends in an action.
3. **Product & validation** – show, don't tell: live demo URL, screenshots of the three screens, Maria's story end to end, **number of real interviews and one real quote** (placeholders in the docx must be filled by David/Megha).
4. **Go-to-market** – first customers: adult children in Stuttgart with parents 70+ living alone. Channels: Pflegestützpunkte, caregiver communities, home-help providers, referrals. Business model: free for seniors, family subscription, provider subscription later. Waitlist sign-ups from the landing page. Stuttgart → Germany → Italy.
5. **Why us / why now** – voice AI finally works for older adults; fragmented care needs one coordinator; the team covers voice agents, full-stack, automation, design, customer discovery. Would we go full-time: answer honestly.

## Demo facts the deck may claim (true today)

- Senior talks to the assistant in German or Italian with one button.
- Emergency detected three ways (AI, keyword net, button); family alerted by SMS and call within seconds; relative confirms; senior sees "Anna is on it".
- Help requests from a conversation appear on the family's phone.
- Runs on Vercel + Supabase (EU) + ElevenLabs + Twilio.

## Claims the deck must NOT make

- That Nonnina calls 112 or is an emergency service (it is not; see `02-emergency-and-family-flow.md`).
- Benefit amounts beyond the checked table in the PDF (Part 5) and `05-market-and-evidence.md`.
- Medical or legal outcomes. Wording: "orientation, not legal or medical advice".
